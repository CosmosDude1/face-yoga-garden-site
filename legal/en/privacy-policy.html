1. Data controller and contact

  • App: Face Yoga Garden
  • Data controller: Arda Meydan (individual developer)
  • Privacy requests: faceyogacozy0@gmail.com

2. Short summary

  • Camera-based expression tracking happens on your device. We do not send or store session camera images, biometric templates, or live tracking data on our servers.
  • Garden, session, streak, companion, and safety data stay on your device by default.
  • An account is optional and is created only with Sign in with Apple on iPhone. We do not offer email/password registration or login.
  • There are no ads, ad tracking, or data sales.

3. Data we process

3.1 Camera and facial movement

The front camera is used on your device for immediate feedback during face-yoga movements. Camera images and the immediate movement measurements derived from them never leave your device. The image is not written to disk, and it is not used for identification or biometric verification.

3.2 Guest use

The core guest experience is local: garden progress and safety responses remain on the device. If you start optional photo analysis, we create a pseudonymous technical guest profile in Supabase only to manage the analysis allowance, purchase and abuse limit. It contains no name or email, does not back up your progress, and can be deleted in Settings.

3.3 Apple account and sync

If you use Sign in with Apple, we create an account using the stable user identifier Apple provides and either your real or private relay email, depending on your choice. We do not request your full name. When you choose sync, progress, mood entries, personal roadmap, collections, and companion preferences are backed up in Supabase. Safety questions and contraindication responses are not synced and remain on the device.

3.4 Optional photo analysis

Personal photo analysis can be started by a guest or a user signed in with Apple. With your explicit consent, one selected selfie is sent once to Google Gemini API to create a personal practice plan. While it is prepared, the result stays only in app memory; if Premium is not purchased, it is not shown or retained. We do not store or sync the photo on our own servers, and it is not used for identification or biometric verification. The App works fully without this feature.

Photo-journal images remain only in the local database on your device and are never uploaded.

3.5 Technical records

For photo analysis, we retain the technical-guest or Apple-account user identifier and request timestamp to prevent abuse; we do not retain photo content. A device diagnostic log reaches us only if you choose to attach it to a support email.

5. Transfers and service providers

  • Supabase is our data processor for Apple-account sync and technical guest profiles/analysis allowance; the hosting region is EU, Frankfurt.
  • Google Gemini API is used only for the photo analysis you explicitly approve. This is an international data transfer.
  • Adapty processes subscription status and, where applicable, the user identifier needed to associate it with an account. It does not receive name, photo, camera data, or advertising identifier.
  • We do not see or store payment-card information; payments are processed by the App Store or Google Play.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Local data remains on your device until you reset it in the App or uninstall the App.
  • Sign out clears the local garden, safety answers, face calibration, and photo journal so the next person using the device cannot access them; the cloud backup associated with your account is not deleted.
  • Synced account data remains while the account is active. The Delete account action in Settings immediately deletes the account, synced progress, analysis counters, the account-linked Adapty profile, and the device photo journal. Users who also want to remove the Sign in with Apple connection are shown iPhone Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple → Face Yoga Garden → Delete and Apple's official support link in the deletion confirmation; this separate Apple Account step does not delay deletion of the App account or its data. For a technical guest profile, Withdraw AI processing consent and delete my guest data deletes that profile, its analysis counter, its Adapty profile, and device data, and stops future photo analysis. We do not retain a copy of a photo previously sent once to Gemini on our servers, so there is no copy for us to recall.
  • Deleting an account does not automatically cancel a subscription; you can manage or cancel it separately in App Store or Google Play settings.

7. Your rights

You may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability, or withdrawal of consent at faceyogacozy0@gmail.com. Where GDPR applies, you also have the rights provided by it. We respond within the period required by applicable law.

8. Children and changes

The App is not directed at people under 18. If we make a material change to this policy, we will notify you in the App.
